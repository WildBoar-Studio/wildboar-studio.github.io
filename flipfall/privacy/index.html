Privacy Policy — Flip Fall

Last updated: September 21, 2026 · Draft — pending App Store release

Wild Boar Studios ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Flip Fall mobile application. This page describes how the app handles data. The app does not require account creation or login.

Information We Collect

We do not directly collect, store, or process personal information on our own servers. Game progress, scores, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

Flip Fall uses the following third-party services, which may collect information as described in their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — Displays advertisements, including rewarded video ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple Game Center — Provides leaderboards and achievements, using your Apple ID. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple StoreKit — Processes in-app purchases. Transactions are handled entirely by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads. You may be asked to consent to ad personalization via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Children's Privacy

Flip Fall does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains no user-generated content or social features beyond optional Game Center leaderboards.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
