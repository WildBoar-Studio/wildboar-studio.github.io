Support

Need help with one of our apps, found a bug, or have a feature idea? We read every message.

Contact us

Email support@wildboarstudios.io and please include:

  • Which app you're using (The Wind Runner, Selah, Flip Fall, or YapIt)
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • A short description of what happened (screenshots help!)

We typically reply within a couple of business days.

Common questions

How do I restore a purchase or subscription?

Open the app's Store or Settings screen and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used at purchase. Subscriptions are managed in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. Refund requests go through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How is my data handled?

Privacy is a priority across our apps. See each app's policy: The Wind Runner, Selah, Flip Fall, YapIt.

How do I delete my data?

Most of our apps store your content on your device, so deleting the app removes it. For apps with a backend account tied to an anonymous ID, email us to request deletion of any server-side data.
